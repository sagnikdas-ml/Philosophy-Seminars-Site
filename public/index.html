Manuscript details

Isaac Newton, MS Add. 3958, Cambridge University Library. Newton Papers; England, c. 1665–1672; English and Latin; paper, 96 ff. From the Portsmouth Collection, donated in 1872. ARK: ark:/12090/cula/84mqfh0n4.

Subjects: Science — Mathematics. Donor: Isaac Newton Wallop, Earl of Portsmouth (1825–1891). Record by Michael Hawkins, Yvonne Santacreu, Robert Iliffe, and Huw Jones.

Bibliography: A. Rupert Hall and Marie Boas Hall (eds), Unpublished Scientific Papers of Isaac Newton (1962); John Herivel, The Background to Newton’s Principia (1965); D. T. Whiteside (ed.), The Mathematical Papers of Isaac Newton, vol. 1 (1967).
